# STM32U575

## Purpose

Main application processor.

Responsible for:

- NFC communication
- Display rendering
- Flash management
- Power management

---

## Why STM32U575?

Selected because:

- Ultra-low-power MCU
- Large Flash memory
- TrustZone support
- Previous development experience
- Mature STM32 ecosystem

---

## Interfaces

SPI

I2C

UART

GPIO
