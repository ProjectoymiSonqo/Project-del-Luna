# Waveshare 7.3-inch Spectra 6

## Purpose

Primary user interface.

Displays digital Goshuin,
travel memories,
illustrations,
and shrine information.

---

## Why Spectra 6?

Selected because:

- Full-color E-paper
- Paper-like appearance
- Extremely low power
- Suitable for long-term display

---

## Why Not LCD?

LCD requires continuous power.

Project del Luna is intended to preserve memories,
not continuously refresh animations.

E-paper better matches the philosophy of the project.

---

## Why 7.3 inches?

Large enough to display:

- Full Goshuin artwork
- Shrine information
- Travel records
- Illustrations

while maintaining a book-like reading experience.
